Rating Guide

Taste

How pleasant is the immediate and after taste? Does it leave an acrid or bitter flavor? Is there depth — can you taste floral or earthy notes? Is it interesting? Due to the nature of carbonated and/or pasteurized beverages, they often score lower in this category than their non-carbonated peers.

1–3 The flavor is unpleasant — there is an issue with the taste that makes me not want to finish the drink.
3–5 The flavor is mediocre or unremarkable — it does not taste bad but isn't particularly interesting, complex, or pleasant.
5–7 There is some depth or complexity, and generally the flavors are balanced, but there is still something to be desired.
7–10 The coconut water is not only pleasant, but also interesting, balanced, and leaves a satisfying aftertaste.

Sweetness

How sweet is the drink? Maturity of coconut, added sugar, and other factors play a role.

1–3 The lack of sweetness is unpleasant — the drink is not balanced.
3–5 The drink is not particularly sweet, but the lack of sweetness is not an issue.
5–7 The drink is sweet, but the sweetness is not an issue.
7–10 The drink is too sweet — the sweetness detracts from the flavor.

Body

How much weight or creaminess the coconut water has in the mouth. Generally, this has a negative correlation with refreshment.

1–3 The texture of the drink is hardly discernible from pure water — very thin.
3–5 There is a slight body, but it doesn't coat the mouth and still feels somewhat delicate.
5–7 The drink has substance — there is clear creaminess.
7–10 The drink feels creamier than whole milk — it coats your palate.

Refreshment

How does the drink quench thirst?

1–3 The texture of the drink is far too creamy to be satisfying.
3–5 While still pleasant to drink when thirsty, it is not particularly thirst quenching.
5–7 The drink has similarly refreshing qualities when compared to water.
7–10 Drinking this is more refreshing than drinking water.

Ethics

What is the impact of producing this coconut water? Large scale coconut farming leads to habitat loss, and abusive labor practices including monkey slavery (for real). This rating exists to see if companies care enough about their impact to talk about it.

1–3 There is little to no information about how the coconut water is sourced.
3–5 Some steps are being taken — there might be some organic or fair-trade ingredients.
5–7 Additional level of organic and fair-trade ingredients, or regenerative, or Rainforest Alliance certified.
7–10 Comprehensive combination of the above.

Overall score is a personal rating based on the water as a whole — it is not an average.

These coconuts are grown in habitats threatened by palm oil deforestation. If you find this site useful, consider donating to the Rainforest Action Network.
