Notes

A Thai coconut water with a smidge of added sugar and pulp.

Verdict

I’m a firm believer that some decent pulp can turn a bad coconut water into a mediocre coconut water. That is the case here — this stuff isn’t good, it tastes like chemicals somehow. But the pulp makes it a bit more palatable, so I’d take it over regular C2O any day of the week.
