Notes

Young Thai coconut water, 100% juice, unpasteurized and USDA Organic. This tastes identical to the Trader Joe’s Organic coconut water — I strongly suspect these come from the same factory.

Verdict

Perfectly balanced between floral and sweet. Refreshing enough without losing too much body. Overall a really solid water. Not the best pick if you like earthiness or a hint of bitterness. See also: Trader Joe’s Organic, which I believe is the same product.
