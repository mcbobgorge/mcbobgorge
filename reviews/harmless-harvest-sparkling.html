Harmless Harvest — Organic Sparkling Coconut Water

Notes

The OG pink water, but carbonated. Harmless Harvest has industry leading ethics (though still not perfect), and this is their classic unpasteurized offering, with bubbles. It is not 100% juice — they've added 16% water to make it a bit less punchy.

Verdict

While it is a great beverage, I don't think Harmless Harvest's crisp, punchy, floral flavor vibes well with carbonation. The best bubbly options are typically milkier, but I appreciate that this exists.
