Notes

This company donates 10% of profits to charity, which is pretty cool, but for some reason they don’t list what charity. Can’t find anything on their website about it. A little bit of added sugar.

Verdict

Unlike most pasteurized waters, this one doesn’t taste cooked. It does, however, taste far too sweet and surprisingly artificial. It’s good as a dessert water but it’s not particularly refreshing.
