Notes

This stuff has very little going for it. Thai coconuts, pasteurized, with some sugar added. At least it is mostly coconut water, and I like the can — it feels sturdy. But it is worse for the planet than a tetra-pak. At least it is recyclable.

Verdict

Honestly impressive for a natural product to taste so artificial. Not only does it have zero body but it also manages to not go down easy thanks to a putrid, bile-y aftertaste. Gross!
