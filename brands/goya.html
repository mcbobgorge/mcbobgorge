Goya Coconut Water Reviews

I have bought and scored 3 Goya coconut waters, listed best to worst by my overall score. My highest was Goya Original at 4.7/10, which places it 19th of 35 overall. My lowest was Goya w/ Pulp at 4.4/10.

ProductOverallIn short
Goya — Coconut Water4.7Goya's Vietnamese coconut water, pasteurized with a hint of added sugar. Standard inoffensive option from one of the largest global food conglomerates.
Goya Organics — Coconut Water4.6Goya's organic coconut water, which still manages to have added sugar. Acrid and gray-tasting despite the sweetness.
Goya — Coconut Water with Pulp4.4Goya's Thai coconut water with pulp and a massive 12g of added sugar per 8oz. Covers bland flavor with sweetness. Solid pulp texture.
