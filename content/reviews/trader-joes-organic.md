## Notes

Young Thai coconut water, 100% juice, unpasteurized and USDA Organic. This tastes identical to the Sprouts Farmers Market Organic coconut water — I strongly suspect these come from the same factory.

## Verdict

Perfectly balanced between floral and sweet. Refreshing enough without losing too much body. Overall a really solid water. Not the best pick if you like earthiness or a hint of bitterness to your water. This is clearly TJ’s response to the success of Harmless Harvest. It is a noble competitor — although I do not think it is a white-label. See also: <a href="sprouts-organic.html">Sprouts Organic</a>, which I believe is the same product.
