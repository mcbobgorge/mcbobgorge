## Notes

I love a good Nam Hom water. Thai coconuts are hard to beat, and this is one of the few pasteurized options in the space. Zico also sells an unpasteurized product that I have yet to review.

## Verdict

Bright flavor profile. Undoubtedly still pasteurized, but they manage to maintain a semblance of the fresh and delicate Nam Hom flavor profile. Quite refreshing! Room for improvement when it comes to the aftertaste but this beats the vast majority of your tetra-pak options.
