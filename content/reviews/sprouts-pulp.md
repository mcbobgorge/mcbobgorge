## Notes

Sprouts makes a bunch of coconut waters, this is their standard pasteurized option with pulp.

## Verdict

Solid but truly not exceptional in any way. The highlight might be the quality pulp. Often, coconut pulp will be too crunchy. This is a nice balance of crunch without feeling like you’re biting rind. But overall unremarkable.
