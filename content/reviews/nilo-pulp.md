## Notes

A Vietnamese coconut water with higher than usual natural sugar — but nothing added besides pulp.

## Verdict

I didn’t have high expectations for this one, but it was surprisingly smooth and mellow. Pasteurized but not burnt, it had a fresh taste. The pulp was low grade and mildly detracted from the drink, but I still walked away satisfied.
