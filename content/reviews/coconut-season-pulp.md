## Notes

Thai coconut water with a very sketchy ingredient list. Water added, as well as preservatives and 15g added sugar per 8oz.

## Verdict

I was expecting this to be a lot sweeter because there is so much sugar, but I think the fact that they add water to dilute it makes it manageably sweet. The pulp is okay, quite firm. But it's a very plain and remarkably unimpressive option.
