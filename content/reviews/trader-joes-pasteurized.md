## Notes

This is a Brazilian coconut water, 100% juice, pasteurized and packaged in a tetra pak.

## Verdict

This is a very mature tasting water, with an almost burnt flavor. Neither sweet nor particularly refreshing, it’s a disappointing offering. I’d strongly recommend heading to the refrigerated section and grabbing TJ’s unpasteurized offering.
