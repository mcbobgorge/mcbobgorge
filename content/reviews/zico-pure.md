## Notes

Zico’s answer to Harmless Harvest. Organic Nam Hom water from Thailand. Unpasteurized.

## Verdict

Better tasting than boxed Zico, but not on the level of some of its other pink rivals. Just a bit too sweet and lacking some of the earthy depth you get from other brands. I wonder if this is pressure treated instead of filtered. Nonetheless a good option if everything else is pasteurized.
