The Best Thai Coconut Water

I have scored 15 coconut waters from Thailand. This page ranks just the Thai ones by my overall score. The highest is Harmless Harvest — Organic Coconut Water at 8.1/10; the lowest is C2O — Coconut Water Original at 2.9/10. Origin is recorded where my review names it; 7 of the 35 reviews do not have one yet.

The Ranking

#ProductOverallIn short
1Harmless Harvest — Organic Coconut Water8.1Harmless Harvest's classic unpasteurized Thai coconut water. Crisp, punchy, and floral with no aftertaste. Industry-leading ethics.
2Kirkland Signature — Organic Coconut Water7.9Costco's Kirkland Signature organic coconut water. Unpasteurized Thai coconut, fruity and bright, very refreshing. Likely related to the TJ's and Sprouts organic offerings.
3Sprouts — Organic Coconut Water7.8Sprouts Farmers Market organic coconut water. Young Thai coconut, unpasteurized, USDA Organic. Likely shares a source with Trader Joe's Organic — they taste identical.
4Trader Joe's — Organic Coconut Water7.8Young Thai coconut water, unpasteurized, USDA Organic. Perfectly balanced between floral and sweet.
5Zico Pure — Organic Coconut Water6.9Zico's answer to Harmless Harvest. Organic unpasteurized Nam Hom water from Thailand. Better than boxed Zico but a bit too sweet and lacking earthy depth.
6Zico — Natural 100% Coconut Water6.6Thai Nam Hom coconut water, pasteurized but brighter than most tetra-pak options. Quite refreshing.
7Taste Nirvana — Real Premium Coconut Water5.4Thai Nam Hom coconut water, steam sterilized. Claims to be the most delicious on the market. Not organic because Thai farmers won't pay for certification.
8C2O — Organic Coconut Water4.8C2O's organic Thai coconut water with some added sugar. Better than the standard C2O but still tastes burnt and too sweet.
9Goya — Coconut Water with Pulp4.4Goya's Thai coconut water with pulp and a massive 12g of added sugar per 8oz. Covers bland flavor with sweetness. Solid pulp texture.
10C2O — Coconut Water with Pulp4.2A Thai coconut water with a smidge of added sugar and pulp. The pulp makes a noticeably chemical-tasting base more palatable.
11Cocochito — with Pulp4.1A heavily sweetened Thai water with added pulp. Bitter and sweet at the same time, with highly variable pulp.
12El Mexicano — Agua de Coco with Pulp3.9A Thai coconut water pasteurized with a bunch of added sugar. Super smooth but milky, with zero freshness or green floral character.
13Coconut Season — Coconut Water with Pulp3.7Thai coconut water with added water, preservatives, and 15g of added sugar per 8oz. Diluted enough to be manageably sweet, but plain and unimpressive.
14Parrot — Coconut Water with Pulp3.1A Thai coconut water with an absurd amount of added sugar and pulp. Candy-sweet with a nice consistent pulp texture, but the sweetness is overwhelming.
15C2O — Coconut Water Original2.9Thai coconut water in a sturdy can, pasteurized with added sugar. Impressively artificial for a natural product.
