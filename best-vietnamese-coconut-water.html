The Best Vietnamese Coconut Water

I have scored 9 coconut waters from Vietnam. This page ranks just the Vietnamese ones by my overall score. The highest is Coaqua — Sparkling at 7.3/10; the lowest is Visvita — Coconut Water with Pulp at 3.8/10. Origin is recorded where my review names it; 7 of the 35 reviews do not have one yet.

The Ranking

#ProductOverallIn short
1Coaqua — Sparkling7.3Coaqua's smooth, milky profile works well with carbonation. Very lightly effervescent. Ethics remain a problem.
2Coaqua — Still6.6Single-origin Vietnamese coconut water with millennial marketing to match. Smooth and milky but added sugar makes it less refreshing.
3Moon Boo Sun — Organic Coconut Water6.2Upmarket organic Vietnamese coconut water from the Ben Tre region. Creamy mouthfeel with mild fruitiness, but a burnt note from pasteurization limits the freshness.
4Nilo — Coconut Water with Pulp5.3A Vietnamese coconut water with higher than usual natural sugar but nothing added besides pulp. Surprisingly smooth and mellow with a fresh, not-burnt taste.
5Real Coco — Organic Pure5.2Mildly sweet organic Vietnamese water. Very inoffensive, almost waterlike, but surprisingly refreshing.
6Goya — Coconut Water4.7Goya's Vietnamese coconut water, pasteurized with a hint of added sugar. Standard inoffensive option from one of the largest global food conglomerates.
7Maui & Sons — 100% Pure4.6Not from Maui. Vietnamese pasteurized coconut water with a slightly burnt flavor and a boring profile.
8Blue Monkey — Organic Pure Coconut Water4.4Pasteurized organic Vietnamese coconut water. No added sugar, tastes burnt but not bad. Zero freshness, great if you don't like sweet waters.
9Visvita — Coconut Water with Pulp3.8Mysterious Vietnamese coconut water with a ton of added sugar and some pulp. Almost soda-like sweetness blasts any flavor out of the picture.
